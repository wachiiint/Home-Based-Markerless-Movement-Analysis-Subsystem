Read this alongside the numbers

    No pass/fail verdict is given, and none is coming until we know how much two recordings of the same leg disagree by. Until that test-retest figure exists, a threshold cannot tell asymmetry apart from recording noise. The symmetry angle is Zifchock's, bounded at ±50%; positive means the left leg's value is larger.
